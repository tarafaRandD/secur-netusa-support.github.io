# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the OWPG Vehicle Checkout iOS app ("the App") handles information while providing vehicle checkout and trip history functionality.

## 1. Overview

The App is an operational tool for recording vehicle activity. It allows an authorized user to select a checkout reason, enter or scan a vehicle beacon ID, record an active trip, and submit the trip information to the App's server.

The App and server do **not** collect or store personally identifiable information (PII), including a person's name, email address, telephone number, account credentials, or other direct identifiers.

## 2. Information the App Collects

### Location data

While a vehicle is actively checked out, the App collects approximate or precise location updates when location services are enabled on the device. Location data may include:

- Latitude and longitude
- Date and time of each location update
- Optional speed information
- Device-generated identifiers and vehicle beacon identifiers needed to associate the trip with the checkout record

The App requests location access so it can record trip history during an active checkout and send that history to the server when the vehicle is returned.

The App does not collect location data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

### Vehicle and trip data

The App may process the following operational data:

- Vehicle beacon or tag ID
- Checkout reason, such as Test Drive, Maintenance, Relocation, or Other
- Checkout and check-in timestamps
- Trip-location history
- Device-generated identifiers used only to associate app operations with a trip
- Request status and correlation information

These identifiers and trip details are not linked to PII and are used only to provide checkout and trip history functionality.

### Camera data

The App uses the device camera to scan vehicle barcodes or tags. Camera data is processed by the App only to obtain the beacon or tag identifier. The App does not use the camera for facial recognition or to collect photos or videos.

## 3. Information the App Does Not Collect

The App does not intentionally collect:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Telephone numbers
- Postal addresses
- Health or fitness data
- Financial or payment information
- Biometric data
- Contacts
- Photos or videos
- Browser history
- Information from third-party apps or websites

The App does not use cookies, web beacons, advertising identifiers, analytics tracking, or other tracking technologies to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information collected or processed by the App is used only to:

- Confirm vehicle checkout and return actions
- Display and maintain active checkout status
- Record and transmit trip-location history
- Associate trip history with the appropriate vehicle and checkout
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service
- Produce anonymized operational or development records when needed

The App does not use collected data for advertising or marketing.

## 5. Device Storage and Trip Data

The App stores active trip-location data on the device so it can build and send the trip history. Each trip overwrites the previous trip's local location file rather than keeping separate, permanent trip history on the device.

The App keeps location data only for the duration of the active trip and the time needed to transmit it to the server. After the trip is completed and the trip data is processed, the local location file is cleared. Deleting the App or clearing App data also removes the local App data stored on the device.

The App's server may retain trip-history records as needed to provide trip history and operational functionality. Server retention periods are determined by the organization operating the service. If the App is removed or a checkout is no longer active, previously saved server trip history may continue to remain in the service until it is deleted under the organization's retention process.

## 6. Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not sell user data, use it for advertising or marketing, or share it with third parties.

The App does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data and does not transmit operational data to external entities for independent use.

Trip and location data may be transmitted only to the App's server and used to provide the App's checkout and trip history functionality. The server is operated for the organization that provides the App, and access is restricted to authorized operational and development personnel as needed.

The server may be required to disclose anonymized or operational data if legally required, for example by a valid legal process or to protect the security of users and systems. Such disclosure, if required, will be limited to what applicable law permits.

## 7. Server Storage and Development Use

The developers may occasionally store anonymized App or server data for debugging, troubleshooting, or development purposes. Such records do not intentionally contain PII and are used only to diagnose or improve operation of the service.

When data is stored for development purposes, developers take reasonable measures to protect it, limit access, and delete it when it is no longer needed. Development data is not used for advertising or marketing and is not shared with third parties.

## 8. Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information processed by the App and server. These measures may include access controls, secure communications, credential protection, and deletion of data that is no longer needed.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. The organization cannot guarantee absolute security, but it works to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 9. Background Location

When the App is handling an active checkout, it may request permission to continue recording trip locations in the background or while the device is locked. Location tracking is intended to stop when the vehicle is returned or the active checkout is ended.

Background location is used solely for trip history. The user can change location permission in iOS Settings, but declining or revoking location access may limit trip recording or other App features.

## 10. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally collect data from third-party sources and does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

The App uses the organization's backend service to provide checkout and trip history functionality. The policy governing that service is this Privacy Policy. Any future integration with an additional service that processes user or trip data will require review and an update to this policy before the integration is enabled.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for organization personnel and operational use. It is not directed to children, and it is not intended to collect PII from anyone, including children.

## 12. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Local trip-location data:** retained while the trip is active and then overwritten or cleared after completion.
- **Server trip-history data:** retained only as needed for operational trip history and service administration.
- **Development or debugging records:** deleted when no longer needed for debugging or development purposes, subject to security and legal requirements.
- **Operational identifiers:** retained only as needed to associate vehicle checkout records and trip history.

To request correction or deletion of server-held operational records, contact the organization operating the App. Deleting the App removes local App data but does not by itself delete server trip-history records; a server-side request may be required.

## 13. Your Choices and Controls

You may control certain App permissions in iOS Settings, including:

- Camera access
- Location access while the App is in use
- Always or background location access, where offered

Camera access is required to scan tags. Location permission is required to record location during an active checkout. Revoking permissions may prevent the App from providing some functionality.

The organization may also restrict or disable user access through its operational service.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's data practices or legal requirements change. When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this policy will be revised, and an updated copy will be made available in the App Store product listing or in the App where appropriate.

The App's developers or the organization operating the service should also review this policy whenever location handling, server storage, SDK integrations, account handling, or checkout functionality changes.
